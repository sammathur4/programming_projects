## Weekly checkpoints — pass/fail, no judgement calls

Every Sunday, three lines in a sheet:

1. **Mock score** (out of 200) — rising trend over 4 weeks = pass
2. **Mains answers written** (target 2) — 2 = pass
3. **Sub-topics self-tested** (target 1 of the 12) — 16/20 correct = pass

Two fails in a row on the same line = that week's plan is wrong, change it. Do not add
more material to fix a scoring problem.

## Do not

- Do not chase predicted questions. 90% of named things in the bank appear in only one year.
- Do not practise elimination tricks. Measured at 22% — below guessing.
- Do not use pre-2023 mocks as your main practice. The question format changed in 2023.
- Do not add a fourth plan document. This page + the 5-day plan is the whole system.
