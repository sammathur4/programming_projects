# UPSC CSE 2027 — integrated 5-day plan (v3)

Target: **complete GS + Sociology optional first pass before Prelims**, then only revision/mocks. No new learning after Prelims.

## Core principles
1. **Integrated study** — one GS topic covers Prelims facts + Mains angles + answer practice. No separate Prelims/Mains study.
2. **Paper-wise practice** — tests rotate GS1 → GS2 → GS3 → GS4, mixed subjects inside each paper. Prelims MCQs are mixed GS Paper 1 sets, not subject-wise.
3. **CSAT is strong** — only PYQs, 10/day. No concept classes.
4. **Daily mains + optional** — 10 mains outlines + 2 optional outlines every workday; full answers on weekends.
5. **Complete before Prelims** — GS first pass by Week 14; optional first pass by Week 18; Weeks 19–27 only revision, mocks, cram.

## Workday template (4 h)
| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 1.5 h | GS paper study — read + prelims facts + mains angles |
| 1.0 h | Sociology lecture at 2× (covers ~2 h of a 3 h lecture) + 2 optional Q |
| 0.5 h | Current affairs — 1.5 h video at 2× (only videos from 9 Nov 2026 onward) |
| 0.5 h | 25 mixed prelims MCQs |
| 0.5 h | 10 mains questions as outlines |
| commute | 10 CSAT PYQs |

**Every day:** CSAT + CA + Sociology + one GS paper. Sociology gets **9 h/week** (5 h workdays + 2.5 h Sat + 1.5 h Sun).

## Weekend template
**Saturday (10 h)**
- 3 h — 1 essay
- 2 h — GS paper test (rotates GS1/GS2/GS3/GS4) + analysis
- 2.5 h — Sociology lecture at 2×
- 1 h — 3 full mains answers
- 1 h — 50 mixed prelims MCQs
- 1 h — monthly CA compilation + weekly review

**Sunday (8 h)**
- 3 h — full prelims mock + analysis
- 2 h — GS revision / weak areas
- 1.5 h — Sociology revision
- 1 h — 3 full mains answers
- 1 h — error log + next-week planning

## Subject mapping (paper-wise)
- **GS1:** Art & Culture, Modern History, Post-Independence, World History, Indian Society, Geography, social issues.
- **GS2:** Constitution, Polity, Governance, Social Justice, IR.
- **GS3:** Economy, Agriculture, SciTech, Environment, Security, Disaster.
- **GS4:** Ethics theory + case studies.
- **Prelims:** mixed GS Paper 1 + CSAT PYQs.
- **Optional:** Sociology Paper 1 + Paper 2, parallel on Thursdays and weekends.

## Playlist policy
- **English:** selective only; not scheduled.
- **Economy:** Saarthi IAS Telegram data only.
- **Environment:** notes + few selected videos.
- **CSAT:** PYQs only; no playlists.
- **Current affairs:** only future videos from 9 Nov 2026, 1.5 h at 2×; monthly compilations May 2026 → May 2027.
- **Sociology:** notes + selective videos; not the full 342 h playlist.
- **Interview:** after Mains.

## Non-negotiables
1. No subject-wise tests — always GS paper format.
2. No new syllabus after Prelims — only revision and answer writing.
3. Never skip Sunday mock analysis or error log.
4. If a day slips, cut CA first, then prelims MCQs; never cut mains/optional outlines.
