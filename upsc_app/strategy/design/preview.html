Question

Consider the following statements regarding the Parliamentary Committee on Public Accounts:

1. It consists of 22 members — 15 from the Lok Sabha and 7 from the Rajya Sabha.
2. The Chairman is appointed by the Prime Minister.
3. It examines the appropriation accounts and the finance accounts of the Government of India.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

a1 and 3 only
b1, 2 and 3
c2 and 3 only
d1 only
✓

Mains answer studio · GS1 · 2013 · Q1

Answer sheet

10 marks200 words

Though not very useful from the point of view of a connected political history of South India, the Sangam literature portrays the social and economic conditions of its time with remarkable vividness. Comment.

IntroductionSangam literature = 300 BCE–300 CE, Tamilakam; three Sangams.
BodySocial: kinship, gender, class · Economic: agriculture, trade, ports · Political: kingship, administration.
EvidenceTolkappiyam, Silappadikaram, Pattinappalai, Roman coin hoards, Musiri.
ConclusionComplements political history; limitations of literary sources.
