# 1. The Central Principle

UPSC preparation normally follows:

> Syllabus → Books → Notes → Current Affairs → PYQs → Tests

The 1000-mark system reverses this:

> **PYQs → recurring themes → knowledge modules → answer frameworks → current affairs → timed writing → testing**

The PYQ database becomes the curriculum.

The syllabus becomes the boundary condition.

Books become sources of information rather than the unit of preparation.

# 16. The PYQ Training System

Your 1,563-question database should be converted into five levels.

## Level 1 — Recognition

Can I identify the theme?

## Level 2 — Recall

Can I produce the basic content?

## Level 3 — Structure

Can I create a 10/15-marker framework?

## Level 4 — Timed answer

Can I write it within exam constraints?

A question should be considered **mastered** only after Level 5.
