# UPSC Sociology Optional 2027 — Final Notes Build Plan

## Deliverables

- Detailed, chapter-wise notes for the complete official Paper 1 and Paper 2 syllabus.
- Every chapter linked to exact-wording PYQs from 2013–2025, with year, paper, marks, theme and answer framework.
- Current-affairs examples from November 2023 to November 2026, linked to relevant themes and checked against primary sources wherever possible.
- Thinker-wise explanations, debates, comparisons, Indian illustrations, diagrams/tables, model-answer structures and revision aids.
- Master index, syllabus/PYQ coverage check, consolidated printable PDF, and editable Markdown source files.
- Searchable transcript for every supplied sociology lecture, used to fill and verify the written notes.

## Source priorities

1. Official UPSC syllabus and original question papers.
2. Pranay Agarwal class notes and the supplied lecture recordings/handouts.
3. Standard textbooks and the other supplied topper/coaching notes.
4. Government data/reports and other traceable primary sources for recent examples.

## Build stages

1. Inventory all sources; extract searchable PDF text; identify scanned or damaged documents.
2. Transcribe every lecture locally in an isolated environment and retain a source manifest.
3. Build and verify the two-paper PYQ database for 2013–2025.
4. Draft chapters strictly against the official syllabus; integrate lectures, PYQs and dated current examples.
5. Audit every syllabus line, thinker and PYQ link; flag uncertain attributions rather than inventing facts.
6. Generate the consolidated PDF and inspect its contents, navigation and rendering.

## Working structure

- `01_Paper_1/` and `02_Paper_2/`: final editable chapters.
- `03_PYQ_Banks/`: verified theme-linked question banks.
- `04_Current_Affairs/`: dated, source-linked examples.
- `05_Revision/`: rapid-revision material.
- `06_Lecture_Transcripts/`: lecture transcripts and manifest.
- `_work/`: extracted source text and intermediate build files.

No mark outcome can be guaranteed; the quality target is complete, accurate, revision-ready coverage.
