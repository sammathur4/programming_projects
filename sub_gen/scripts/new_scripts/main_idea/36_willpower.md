# WILLPOWER — GRIT, DETERMINATION
# ------------------------------------------------------------

My willpower is absolute, unbreakable, and permanent.
I do what I said I would do, always.
I turn away from what weakens me, instantly and gladly.
My grit is legendary, and I outlast every obstacle.
I hold the line when everyone else quits.
I choose strength, and I win.
I am determined by default, and it stays on.
I continue through boredom, difficulty, and solitude.
I do the work no one sees, and I win where everyone watches.
Setbacks sharpen me. I am unstoppable.
I am the most determined person I know.
I am law unto myself, and my will holds.
I choose the hard thing right now.
I keep my word today.
I outlast everyone around me.
I am unbreakable in this moment.
