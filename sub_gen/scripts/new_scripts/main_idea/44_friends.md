Invitations to plans arrive constantly.

I connect people and build community.
I have friends in every city and every field.

I am everyone's favourite friend.
People miss me like crazy when I am away.

## LOYALTY THAT NEVER WAVERS

My friends are loyal to me in my presence and in my absence.
They defend my name in rooms I have never entered.
They keep my secrets like their own.
They stand with me when it costs them something.
They show up when I need them, before I ask.
They choose me over convenience, every single time.
My people are ride-or-die, and they prove it.
Not one of them speaks ill of me behind my back.
My circle is small, real, and unbreakable.

## THEY SPEND ON ME

## THEY WORSHIP ME

My friends admire me openly and speak of me with pride.
They quote me, repeat my stories, and laugh before the punchline.

They introduce me as the man to know.
They treat my presence like the best part of any room.

They celebrate my wins louder than I do.

I am the man my friends brag about.

## THEY HELP BEFORE I ASK

My friends help me before I have to ask.

They open doors, make introductions, and solve problems in the background.
They lend money, time, and effort without hesitation.
They carry my load when my hands are full.
